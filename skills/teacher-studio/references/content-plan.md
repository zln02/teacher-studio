# Planning and research content — what makes a one-page plan decision-ready

`co-plan.md` gives the flow and the page order. This file sets the quality of what fills it: real options, dated sources, named risks, a recommendation someone can act on. Wording: `content-writing.md`. Options and criteria come from this decision; do not carry over a previous plan's A/B/C.

## Steps
1. **State the decision.** 「〈누가〉 〈언제까지〉 〈무엇을〉 정해야 한다.」 Research with no decision behind it → ask what it will be used for, or state the assumed use. Write the success condition in one line (how the user will know it worked).
2. **List the constraints first**: 예산 · 시간 · 인원 · 대상 학년 · 장소 · 기기 · 학교 일정 · 필요한 결재. Known ones from the request and prefs; unknown ones that would flip the choice → ask (within co-plan's 5-question limit); the rest → written assumptions.
3. **Generate options that really differ.**
   - Options that differ in approach, not in size (not 소 · 중 · 대 of the same idea). Usually 2-4; a yes/no decision may need two, a complex one more. The count follows the decision.
   - Include the low-effort option (「지금 하던 대로 + 한 가지만 바꾸기」) when it is honest to do so. It is often the right answer.
   - No straw options added to make the favourite look good. If only one option is viable, say so and explain why the others fail.
4. **Compare on the user's criteria.**
   - Choose 3-5 criteria from the constraints and the success condition (예: 준비 시간, 비용, 학생 참여, 위험, 지속 가능성; pick what matters here, not this list).
   - Each option gets: what you gain, what you give up, cost (돈 · 시간 · 사람) with numbers where possible, and what must be true for it to work.
   - Every option shows a real downside. An option with no downside has not been examined.
   - Table when ≥ 3 criteria; otherwise one line each.
5. **Source every factual claim.**
   - Each: 제목 · 기관/저자 · 발행일 or 기준일 · 링크 · 확인한 날짜. Prefer the primary source (법령 · 교육부/교육청 공문 · 통계청 · 기관 보고서) over news, news over blogs; label a blog 「보조 출처」.
   - Check the date against the claim: a rule, price, statistic or product feature older than the current 학년도 gets 「최신 여부 확인 필요」.
   - Match the check to the kind of fact:
     - a rule, a deadline, a 공고, a confirmed 예산 → one source is enough if it is the authority itself (법령 · 고시 · 공문 원문 · 학교 결재 문서), with its 시행일/적용일. Adding a news article to it adds nothing.
     - an estimate, a price, a statistic, an effect claim → look for a second source that is truly independent (not two pages copying the same original). If they disagree, show both.
     - an estimate you made → give the range and the condition under which the recommended option would change.
   - Separate three kinds of statement in the text: 확인한 사실 (출처) · 추정 (근거) · 의견 (누구의). No search tool → say so once and mark claims 「확인 필요」; never invent a source, a quote or a link.
   - Summarise in your own words; quote at most a sentence.
6. **Name the risks concretely.** For each top risk (≤ 3-5): what could go wrong → how likely / how bad (낮음 · 중간 · 높음 with a reason) → the early sign → what to do about it (prevent, or the fallback). Always scan: 개인정보 · 저작권 · 안전 · 예산 초과 · 일정 충돌(시험 · 행사) · 담당자 부담 · 학부모 민원 · 기기/인터넷 고장. Legal ones → `../teacher-law`.
7. **Recommend, with the reasoning and its breaking point.**
   - One option, one-line reason tied to the user's criteria, and what is given up by choosing it.
   - 「이 조건이면 다른 안이 낫다」: state the condition that would change the recommendation.
   - Confidence in plain words (확실 · 대체로 · 자료 부족) and what would raise it.
   - If the evidence does not support a choice, or the options differ too little to tell, say that and recommend the cheapest test that would decide it (one class, two weeks, one measure agreed beforehand).
8. **Turn it into next steps**: 3-5 actions in order, each with 누가 · 언제까지 · 무엇이 나오면 끝인지. The first step is doable this week. Mark steps that need 결재 or someone else's agreement.
9. **Keep the decision readable at a glance.** One page is the default because the decider must see options, risks and the recommendation together; a complex decision may run longer with a one-screen summary on top. Details and the full source list go below a line or in an appendix the user can ignore.

## 전문가 점검표
- [ ] The decision, the decider, the deadline and the success condition are on the page.
- [ ] Options differ in approach; each has a real downside and a cost; no straw option.
- [ ] Criteria came from the user's constraints, and the comparison uses the same criteria for all options.
- [ ] Every fact has a dated source and a 확인한 날짜; rules and deadlines come from the authoritative text with its 적용일; decision-critical estimates have a second independent source or a stated range; old facts flagged.
- [ ] A rule, deadline, cost or eligibility the plan depends on that could not be confirmed is named as the first next step (「먼저 확인할 것」), not hidden and not stamped across the plan.
- [ ] Fact · 추정 · 의견 are distinguishable; nothing invented; gaps marked 「확인 필요」.
- [ ] Risks have early signs and responses; privacy/copyright/safety were scanned.
- [ ] The recommendation states what is given up and what would change it; confidence is stated.
- [ ] Next steps have owner · date · done-condition; the first is doable this week.
- [ ] No student data or contact details; placeholders only.
- [ ] Summary fits one screen; length beyond that is justified by the decision; reads as written for this user's situation, not a generic framework.
