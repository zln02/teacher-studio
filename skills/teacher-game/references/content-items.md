# Item quality — questions, options, explanations at an 출제 전문가 level

`content.md` gives the basic rules (source, length, 확인 필요). This file adds what an experienced 출제 · 검토 교사 checks. Lesson fit (목표 · 오개념 · 수준) comes from `../teacher-studio/references/content-lesson.md`. Write items from this unit's objectives; do not reuse item shapes from an example with the nouns changed.

## First: in-class check or graded assessment?
- **수업 중 확인 · 복습 게임 (default for this skill):** the goal is learning and deciding the next teaching move. A wrong option chosen is a clue, not proof, of a misconception: plan a follow-up (「왜 그렇게 골랐어요?」 or a second item on the same idea). Speed, teams and luck are fine.
- **성적에 들어가는 평가:** a game is the wrong instrument (speed, tapping, colour and team effects change the score). If the teacher wants items for a graded test, write them as a draft and add what a graded assessment needs: 가르친 범위 확인 · 채점 기준과 배점 · 허용 답안과 부분 점수 · 평가 조정 · 교차 검토 (`../teacher-studio/references/content-lesson.md` step 12). Say that the school's 학업성적관리규정 decides the procedure.

## Steps
1. **List what is being checked** before writing any item: the 2-5 objectives or key ideas, and for each the kind of thinking (기억 · 이해 · 적용 · 판단). Decide the spread on purpose.
   - 복습 · 도입 5분 → mostly 기억 · 이해, fast.
   - 단원 정리 · 형성평가 → include items that need the idea used, not only named. Why: a round of pure recall tells the teacher nothing about understanding. About a third 적용 or higher is a starting point; follow the objectives (a 용어 익히기 lesson may rightly be mostly recall, a 문제 해결 lesson mostly 적용).
   - If every item is recall of a single word, say so to the teacher and offer two 적용 items.
2. **Decide what the item measures, then keep it to that.** If answering needs two unrelated facts, split it, because a wrong answer would not tell you which one failed. If the target is combining two pieces of evidence (비교 · 종합 · 판단), keep them together: that combination is the idea. The stem should be answerable before seeing the options (cover-the-options test) unless the task is to choose among given cases.
   - Remove difficulty that is not the target: long reading in a maths item, a trick in wording, a time limit shorter than the thinking, colour-only cues, small touch targets.
3. **Write the stem as a direct question** in one sentence. Conditions, data or a short case go on a second line. Positive wording by default; 「아닌 것」 only when the objective is to spot the exception, and then the negative word is marked.
4. **Build distractors from real errors.**
   - Each wrong option answers: 「어떤 생각을 한 학생이 이것을 고르는가?」 If no student would, it is not a distractor.
   - Sources: the unit's 오개념, a correct fact about a different thing, a half-right procedure (one step skipped, 단위 안 바꿈, 부호 실수), the everyday meaning of a term.
   - Same category, same grammar, similar length as the answer. No 「모두 정답」「정답 없음」. No joke option in an assessment round (one allowed in a warm-up).
   - The correct option is not the longest, the most precise or the only one with a qualifier; its position varies.
5. **Exactly one defensible answer.** Argue for each wrong option for ten seconds as a sharp student would. If the argument holds under some reading, fix the stem or the option. 「가장 알맞은」 does not rescue an ambiguous item.
6. **Write the explanation for the student who got it wrong**: one line of 「왜 맞는지」 plus, where one misconception dominates, why that choice is tempting but wrong. No restating the answer word (the engine prints it).
7. **Order and pace.** Start with one or two that everyone can get; raise difficulty; put the item that exposes the main 오개념 in the middle, not last; end on a solvable one. In team play, keep point values and difficulty in step so luck does not decide.
8. **Vary on purpose**: contexts, question forms (고르기 · 순서 · 짝 · 분류 · 참거짓 with reason), and which objective comes when. Three items in a row with the same shape read as a worksheet.
9. **Verify every key.** Solve each item yourself without looking at the key; recompute numbers by a second route; check 연도 · 단위 · 표기 against the source. Items you could not verify stay out or carry 「확인 필요」 in the review table.
10. **Hand the teacher a review table**: 번호 · 목표 · 문제 · 정답 · 왜 · 확인 필요. Lead with what you were unsure of.

## By game type
| type | content rule |
|---|---|
| quiz / bell | stem short enough to read from the back row before the timer matters; time limit follows the thinking needed (starting points: recall 10-20초, 적용 30-60초; lengthen for 저학년, calculation or reading, and let the teacher turn the timer off) |
| escape room | each lock checks an objective; the story gives a reason for the task, not extra reading; hints teach the step, they do not give the answer |
| matching / word pairs | pairs unambiguous one-to-one; no two left items that fit the same right item; meanings from the teacher's list or the textbook, not invented sentences |
| sorting / drag | categories exclusive and complete for the items given; include one item that tests the boundary and explain it; the same task must be doable without dragging (tap an item, then tap the place; or keyboard) and without telling colours apart. If the engine offers no such way for this type, tell the teacher and offer a tap-based type |
| bingo | call sentences are clues that need the idea, not the word read aloud |

## 전문가 점검표
- [ ] Every item maps to a listed objective; the spread of 기억/이해/적용 was chosen, and reported.
- [ ] In-class check or graded use is stated; nothing from a game is presented as a grade; a graded draft lists 범위 · 채점 기준 · 허용 답안 · 조정 · 교차 검토.
- [ ] Each item measures one stated thing (a single idea, or a deliberate combination); each stem passes the cover-the-options test where it applies.
- [ ] No difficulty from outside the target (reading load, speed, colour, motor skill); every input has a non-drag, non-colour way, or the gap is reported.
- [ ] Each distractor has a named student error behind it; the unit's main 오개념 appears at least once.
- [ ] One and only one defensible answer per item (ten-second devil's advocate done).
- [ ] No cueing: answer not longest/most detailed; grammar of stem fits all options; positions vary; no option repeats a stem word alone.
- [ ] Negative stems ≤ 2 in 10 and marked.
- [ ] Explanations say 「왜」 and address the tempting error.
- [ ] All keys re-solved independently; numbers recomputed; facts sourced. An item whose key you could not verify is left out of the game and named in the review table (CONTENT-RULES, Unknowns: no draft mark inside the file).
- [ ] Difficulty rises, with an easy start and a solvable end; forms and contexts vary.
- [ ] Nothing shames a wrong answer; no real names; contexts free of stereotype.
