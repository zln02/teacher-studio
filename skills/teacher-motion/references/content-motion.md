# Motion content — what the words and beats say (과목 소개 · 수업 오프닝 · 홍보 영상)

Motion makes weak content look finished, so settle the content first. Lesson pieces follow `../teacher-studio/references/content-lesson.md`; promotion follows `../teacher-studio/references/content-promo.md`; every line follows `../teacher-studio/references/content-writing.md`. Write the script for this subject and audience; do not reuse a previous video's beat order with new nouns.

## Steps
1. **One sentence for the whole video**: 「이걸 본 〈누구〉는 〈무엇을 궁금해한다/안다/한다〉.」 A 20-60초 piece holds one idea. A second idea is a second video.
2. **Pick the job**, because it changes the ending.
   | job | ends on |
   |---|---|
   | 수업 오프닝 · 도입 | an open question or prediction the lesson will answer (do not give the answer) |
   | 과목 · 단원 소개 | what students will be able to do, and the first thing they do |
   | 홍보 · 모집 | the call to action with date, place, how |
   | 개인 · 행사 영상 | the moment or the thanks |
3. **Write the script as beats before any animation**: each beat = one line on screen (≤ 15자 preferred, one breath) + what is seen. Read the lines alone in order; they must make sense without the motion.
4. **Front-load.** The first 3 seconds state the subject or the question. No logo-only or title-only opening longer than 2 seconds.
5. **Time for reading.** Hold each line long enough to read twice at the audience's level (rule of thumb: ≈ 0.3초 per 글자, minimum 1.5초; longer for 초 저학년). If the lines do not fit the duration, cut lines, not hold time.
6. **Words and picture share the load.** Do not show a sentence and animate a different idea at the same time. Emphasis (크기 · 색 · 움직임) falls on the word that carries the meaning, one per beat.
7. **Works without sound and without motion.** Every fact is on screen as text; a paused frame at the end shows the message or CTA complete.
8. **Facts and rights**: numbers, dates, names verified (CONTENT-RULES rule 4); music, fonts, images, faces have a basis (`../teacher-law` when unsure).

## 전문가 점검표
- [ ] One idea; the one-sentence purpose is met by the last frame.
- [ ] Script lines read alone make sense and follow in order.
- [ ] Subject or question appears within 3 seconds.
- [ ] Each line can be read twice in its hold time; no beat has two competing emphases.
- [ ] 오프닝 ends on a question the lesson answers; 홍보 ends on a complete CTA held ≥ 3초.
- [ ] No empty lines (「지금 시작합니다」「함께 떠나요」) that fit any topic.
- [ ] Understandable muted and as a still of the final frame.
- [ ] Facts verified; dates with computed 요일; rights cleared and credited.
