# Motion prompt examples (참고 요청문 몇 가지)

A short, curated set of motion / explainer request shapes, **adapted** for school and everyday use from the community collection
yihui-dev/awesome-opus5-5-videos (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 yihui-dev; each original prompt is by the creator credited below).
They are not copied: each is re-written for our stage (1920x1080, anime.js, ES2017, offline, no GSAP) and our scene rules
(one idea per shot, real things before analogies, end on a real result, no speed claims). Use them to shape a teacher's one-line
request into a shot list; never paste them as the content.

| # | Adapted request (our words) | What it teaches about structure | Inspired by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 「우리 학교 축제를 알리는 30초 영상: 큰 글자 한 마디 → 그림 한 장 → 날짜 → 프로그램 카드 3개 → 끝 카드(언제·어디서)」 | a launch film: hook, hero, facts, features, call to action; the end card holds 2.5 s | @moritzkremb (product-launch motion graphics) |
| 2 | 「우리 반 1년을 15초 하이라이트로: 장면 6개, 장면마다 한 낱말, 마지막에 반 이름」 | a showreel: fast cuts on one beat, one word per cut, a still last frame | @melvynx (15 s showreel of a site) |
| 3 | 「이 책(또는 수업 도구)을 40초로 소개: 먼저 실제 화면, 그다음 왜 좋은지 세 가지」 | research the real thing first, show its real screen, then the reasons | @ajith_io (40 s product showreel, research first) |
| 4 | 「노래 가사를 종이 오려 붙인 느낌으로 한 줄씩 보여 주기 (음원은 선생님 파일)」 | words timed to an existing track, a paper-cut hand-made look; the user's own audio only | @anabology (lyric video with a hand-drawn paper feel) |
| 5 | 「AI에게 일 맡기는 과정을 1분으로: 입력 화면 → 캐릭터들이 나눠 일하기 → 진짜 결과물」 | a making-of: real input screen, a playful middle, it ends on the real output (no 「몇 초 만에」) | @daniel_mac8 (terminal to animated workflow) |

How to use: pick the row whose structure fits, write the shot table (번호 · 장면 · 화면 글자 · 길이) from the teacher's own facts, then build
with a missile (`event-promo` for row 1) or the STAGE (`SKILL.md` 「미사일이 없을 때 직접 제작」). Characters and music are the user's or
original; no famous characters, brands or lyrics we do not have the rights to (`legal-advisory.md`).
